01

Start with your memory

Create your illustration in ChatGPT first, then add it here. A regular photo works too.

Your ChatGPT illustration brief ↗

Transform this photo into a warm, intimate keepsake illustration. Use soft coloured pencil, subtle watercolour, muted natural colours and a gentle paper feel. Preserve each person's face shape, hairstyle, glasses and distinct expression. Keep the original pose, linked arms, clothing, medals, meaningful objects and location cues. Simplify the actual background. Avoid exaggerated cartoon features, heavy smoothing or busy decoration. Do not add text. Being recognisable matters more than decorative perfection.

Attach your photo in ChatGPT and add the details you need preserved. Review likeness before downloading your illustration. This site does not run AI generation.

02

Find your words

Before you keep it

Check every face, hairstyle, expression, pose and meaningful object against the original. Keep permission from everyone pictured. For four chapters, choose images in top-left, top-right, bottom-left, bottom-right order.

Illustrations are always fitted in full. Small sources may print softly. The preview uses example landscape art until you choose an image.
